# VeloPlayer Privacy Policy **Effective date:** October 24, 2026 ## 1. Introduction VeloPlayer ("the App") is a media player application that allows users to play and manage video and audio files stored on their Android devices. Your privacy is important to us. VeloPlayer is designed to process your media locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, where it is stored, and what choices you have. ## 2. Information We Access ### 2.1 Media Files With the appropriate Android permissions or user-granted file access, VeloPlayer may access audio and video files stored on your device. Depending on the features you use, the App may access media information such as: - File names and file locations or content URIs. - Video and audio duration. - File size and media format. - Audio track and subtitle information. - Media titles, artists, album information, and available artwork. - Other metadata provided by your media files. This information is used to display your media library and provide playback features. VeloPlayer does not require you to upload your personal media files to an external server to play them. ### 2.2 Playback History and Preferences VeloPlayer may store information locally on your device to provide features such as: - Recently played media. - Playback positions and resume functionality. - Favorites and playlists. - Preferred audio and subtitle languages. - Playback settings and preferences. - Themes, custom colors, and player interface settings. This information is used to personalize your experience and maintain your preferences between sessions. ### 2.3 Device Information The App may access certain device information required for normal operation, such as battery status for a user-enabled battery indicator, display configuration for video playback, and audio output information required for playback controls. Android may also display system-managed notifications and media controls when relevant. VeloPlayer does not intentionally collect device identifiers for advertising or user tracking. ## 3. How We Use Information Information accessed by VeloPlayer is used to: - Play local audio and video files. - Organize and display your media library. - Remember playback positions and history. - Manage playlists and favorites. - Select audio tracks and subtitles. - Apply your selected themes and settings. - Provide background audio playback and supported media controls. - Improve playback reliability and provide user-requested features. The App does not use your personal media library for advertising or sell your media information. ## 4. Data Collection and Sharing VeloPlayer is designed to operate locally on your device. The App does not intentionally transmit your media files, playback history, playlists, or customization preferences to the developer or other third parties. The App does not include advertising or analytics services, provided that these remain absent from the distributed version. VeloPlayer does not sell personal information. Android operating-system services and any services you independently use may process information according to their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy does not cover the independent data practices of those services. ## 5. Internet Access VeloPlayer is intended to provide local media playback without requiring an internet connection. If a future version introduces an online feature or a third-party service that requires network access, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data processing before or when that feature becomes available. ## 6. Permissions Depending on your Android version and the features you use, VeloPlayer may request permissions such as: - **Audio and video access:** To find, display, and play supported media files. - **Notifications:** To display playback notifications where applicable. - **Foreground media playback:** To support playback that continues while the App is in the background. - **Wake lock:** To support appropriate playback behavior when the device screen is off. Permission availability and requirements vary by Android version. You can review or revoke permissions through your Android device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent the associated feature from working. ## 7. Data Storage and Retention Playback history, playlists, favorites, and application preferences are intended to be stored locally on your device. This information is retained as needed to provide the relevant features and may remain until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling VeloPlayer does not delete your original media files from shared device storage. Device backups or Android-managed services may handle application data according to your device settings and their own policies. ## 8. Children's Privacy VeloPlayer is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children. If you believe that the App is collecting information it should not, please contact the developer using the contact information below. ## 9. Security VeloPlayer is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection and keep application preferences and playback information on your device. You should keep your Android device updated and use appropriate device security measures. No software or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 10. Your Choices and Controls You can control your use of VeloPlayer through the App's settings and Android system settings. Depending on the available features, you can: - Grant or revoke media permissions. - Clear application data through Android settings. - Remove playlists, favorites, or playback history using available in-app controls. - Customize playback and privacy-related preferences. - Uninstall the App at any time. Deleting application data or uninstalling the App may remove locally stored preferences and playback information. ## 11. Third-Party Services VeloPlayer is not intended to share your personal media library or playback history with third-party advertising or analytics providers. Android operating-system components and device manufacturers may provide system-level functionality required for playback, notifications, permissions, and media controls. Their processing is governed by their own applicable policies. If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated as appropriate. ## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to VeloPlayer, its features, or applicable legal and platform requirements. When the policy changes, the effective date shown at the top will be updated. Users should periodically review this page for the latest version. ## 13.
